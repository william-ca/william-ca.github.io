The most popular lever-action center-fire rifle that Winchester ever produced. It sold new in 1866 for $20.00 and was produced from 1873 to 1919. During this 46 year period there were over 720,000 rifles sold. The Winchester Model 1873 is considered by many to be “The Gun that won the West”.

Family History

“My Grandfather, Fred Carl Erhardt was born in 1877 and died in the year 1966. This rifle was a birthday gift on his 18th birthday in 1895. The rifle at that time was used and 9 years old. The rifle was used several years on his Ada Michigan form for hunting rabbits and rodents. My grandfather told me the last time the rifle was fired was to kill a cow at the Ada Michigan slaughter house in the 1926. My mother Dena Erhardt (Andre) was still living at home on the farm with her parents since she did not marry William Andre (my father) until June 4, 1929.

This rifle was given to me as a gift from my Grandfather in the 1950's. It is my desire that this rifle will always be treated as a gift and to be handed down from generation to generation as a family treasure.

— Carl Fred AndreDated October 22, 2012
